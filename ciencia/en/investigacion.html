Geometric foundations of gravity

This line covers all the aspects related to the modelling of gravity at a more fundamental level. The idea would be to explore new mathematical structures or approaches in order to address unanswared questions in the field.

Analysis of specific solutions and geometries

This line focuses on constructing and analysing geometries of gravitational interest, with particular attention to their global properties, causal structure and topology. It includes the study of toroidal black holes and cosmological spacetimes with sign-changing spatial curvature and topological transitions.

Instabilities in modified gravity

Strongly coupled backgrounds

The aim of this line is to continue with the study of the implications of the so-called strong coupling problem in modified gravity. The problem can be detected by different methods and it is still unclear how these methods are related to each other (are they equivalent? or is some of them more general than the rest?). In some specific backgrounds in teleparallel gravity, this problem is expected to be solved for some solutions that, as a consequence of this, become physically viable spacetimes (at least from the point of view of this instability).

Publications in this direction

  1. J. Beltrán Jiménez, A. Jiménez-Cano,
    On the strong coupling of Einsteinian Cubic Gravity and its generalisations,
    JCAP 01, 069 (2021), arXiv: 2009.08197 [gr-qc]
Stability of quadratic metric-affine gauge(qMAG) gravity

The aim of this line is to understand how restrictive are the stability conditions in metric-affine gravity. We have first focused on the case of propagating vectors, i.e. we assume a qMAG theory propagating at least one of the spin-1 contained in the vector irreducible parts of the torsion and the nonmetricity. We have recently shown that this is enough to restrict from 16 to 5 the number of parameters in the curvature square sector.

Publications in this direction

  1. A. Jiménez-Cano, F. J. Maldonado Torralba
    Vector stability in quadratic metric-affine theories,
    JCAP 09, 044 (2022), arXiv: 2205.05674 [gr-qc]
Teleparallel theories

This line aims to explore stable theories of gravity within the teleparallel framework, based on geometries with torsion and/or nonmetricity, but no curvature.
