Presentation

Welcome to my scientific website. My name is Alejandro Jiménez Cano. I am currently an independent researcher, offering personalised tuition and academic mentoring in general relativity, gravitation and their mathematical foundations. You can find out more about my teaching on Instagram at @alejandro.gravedad.

So far my research has been focused on the general properties and the structure of theories of gravity beyond General Relativity. I have mostly worked on solutions and stability issues of various theories such as cubic gravity and theories formulated in the so-called Metric-Affine framework. I have many interests in the field of gravitation such as junction conditions, black hole stability, TDiff theories and differential geometry.

My previous teaching experience includes General Physics at the Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería de Montes, Forestal y del Medio Natural, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid. I have also contributed to the doctoral course Selected Topics in the Theories of Gravity at the University of Tartu. At the University of Granada, I taught Quantum Mechanics problem-solving classes for Physics students, as well as General Physics problem-solving classes and laboratory sessions across several degree programmes.

In addition, I usually take advantage of any opportunity to participate in informative talks and outreach activities with children, showing them some of the deepest aspects of the modern gravitational theories.
